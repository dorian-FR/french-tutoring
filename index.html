Why learning French?

Everybody has a different reason to learn French.
It could be either:

For school

To understand your French classes better or to prepare for an exam.

For work

To improve your resume or your career.

For going to France

To move there or simply visit the country for holidays.

For your French family, friends, or colleagues

to create with them a smoother connection.

For curiosity

To understand the French language, history, or culture.

And you may already have one or more of these reasons in mind... and don't know who would be able to help you.

Bonjour!

As I now live abroad, I aim to share my knowledge online in an international environment.

Throughout my teaching journey, I helped my students develop their critical thinking, improve their writing and speaking skills, and gain confidence in their learning.
